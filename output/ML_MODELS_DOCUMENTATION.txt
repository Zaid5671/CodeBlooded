================================================================================
               AI-POWERED MPLADS AUDIT INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM (LOK SABHA 18TH)
                  MACHINE LEARNING & STATISTICAL MODEL ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

OVERVIEW:
The MPLADS AI Intelligence System employs an ensemble of unsupervised machine learning,
natural language processing (NLP), peer-relative statistical anomaly detection, and graph/network
analytics to identify potential administrative, financial, operational, and record-linkage anomalies
across 79,220 sanctioned works (and 79,221 total reconciled master entities) in Lok Sabha 18th.

DATASET & RECONCILIATION DISTINCTION:
- Total Sanctioned Work Records: 79,220 (Evaluated across Models 2, 3, 4, 5, and analytical modules).
- Total Reconciled Master Entities: 79,221 (Includes 79,220 sanctioned works plus 1 unsanctioned work
  identified in expenditure records during cross-lifecycle reconciliation).

GOVERNANCE & AUDIT POLICY:
Because ground-truth labels for fraudulent transactions do not exist in standard government datasets,
the system strictly avoids supervised classifiers (e.g. XGBoost, Random Forest with synthetic labels)
to prevent label leakage and false accusations. All algorithms operate via statistical distribution analysis,
record linkage, rule-based statutory auditing, or unsupervised multivariate isolation trees.

SYSTEM CANONICAL ARCHITECTURE:
- 3 AI/ML Components:
  1. Record Linkage & TF-IDF / Embedding Pairwise Matching (Duplicate Work Detector)
  2. Unsupervised Isolation Forest & Hierarchical IQR/MAD Ensemble (Anomalous Cost Estimate Detector)
  3. Time-Series Seasonal Baseline Utilization & Anomaly Flagging (Expenditure Forecasting Model)
- 3 Rule / Analytical Engines:
  4. Non-parametric Tukey IQR Peer Delay & SLA Engine (Delayed Projects Detector)
  5. Statutory 45-Day Administrative Compliance Engine (Compliance Deviation Detector)
  6. Graph Network Concentration & Payment Structuring Risk Engine (Vendor–Agency Network Risk Analyzer)
- 1 Multi-Signal Decision Layer:
  7. Model 5 Audit Priority Aggregator (Synthesizes signals into normalized priority tiers)
- Implemented Analytical Modules:
  8. Inadmissible Works 3-Stage Syntactic Landmark Engine (Module 2)
  9. Private & Commercial Beneficiary Detector (Module 3)
  10. Duplicate Expenditure & Voucher Matching Engine (Module 6)
  11. Fund Utilization & Expenditure Speed Engine (Module 7)

1. MODEL 1 — DOUBLE-DIPPING / POTENTIAL DUPLICATE WORK DETECTOR (RECORD LINKAGE)
================================================================================

OBJECTIVE:
Identify pairs of distinct MPLADS works that possess highly similar physical descriptions, 
overlapping geographical locations, and close execution timelines within the same constituency block,
indicating potential duplicate sanctioning or double billing.

ARCHITECTURAL PIPELINE:
1. Candidate Generation / Blocking:
   - Candidate pairs are generated by blocking on (State, District, Constituency).
   - District is deterministically extracted from the IDA field (e.g. 'JAUNPUR(DISTRICT MAGISTRATE JAUNPUR_IDA)' -> 'JAUNPUR').
   - District Coverage: 79,219 / 79,220 usable records (99.9987%); 1 record falls back to (State, Constituency).
   - Total Raw Blocked Candidate Pairs: 6,954,003 pairs across 1,262 blocks (Largest block: 1,154 works).
   - Generates top 5,000 candidate work pairs after blocking and TF-IDF similarity ranking.

2. Text Normalization & Generic Phrase Removal:
   - Descriptions undergo cleaning: lowercasing, whitespace normalization, removal of boilerplate phrases 
     (e.g., "construction of", "installation of", "supply of", "c.c. road", "paver block").
   - Location-specific identifiers (village names, road codes, school names, survey numbers) are preserved.

3. Vector Embeddings & Similarity Calculation:
   - Primary Backend: TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency) N-gram Token Vectorizer 
     (unigrams & bigrams) paired with Cosine Similarity.
   - Fallback Backend: Pure-Python Jaccard & Token Set Similarity Vectorizer.
   - Dynamic Weighting: Pairs sharing identical generic phrases without unique location tokens are 
     penalized to prevent high-similarity false positives.

4. Multi-Feature Pairwise Risk Scoring:
   - Text Similarity Score (Weight: 35%)
   - Sanction Amount Match Score (Weight: 20%)
   - Location Token Overlap (Weight: 20%)
   - Vendor Matching Score (Weight: 15%)
   - Date Proximity Score (Weight: 10%)

5. Risk Tier Classification:
   - HIGH RISK: Composite Score >= 85 (Requires formal audit review)
   - MEDIUM RISK: Composite Score between 70 and 84
   - LOW RISK: Composite Score < 70

6. Chamber Scope & Cross-House Status:
   - Intra-House (LS-LS) Detection: Active across 79,221 Lok Sabha 18th master entities.
   - Cross-House (LS-RS) Architecture: Fully implemented interface `compare_works(df_a, df_b, chamber_pair)`.
   - Cross-House Status: `PENDING_RAJYA_SABHA_DATA` (`CROSS_HOUSE_ENABLED = False`). Banner displayed on UI.

PRODUCTION METRICS (LS 18th Data):
   • Candidate Pairs Evaluated (LS-LS): 5,000 top ranked pairs (from 6,954,003 raw blocked pairs across 1,262 blocks)
   • HIGH RISK Pairs Flagged (LS-LS): 1,810 pairs
   • MEDIUM RISK Pairs Flagged (LS-LS): 2,142 pairs
   • LOW RISK Pairs Flagged (LS-LS): 1,048 pairs
   • Cross-House Candidates (LS-RS): 0 (Pending Rajya Sabha data)

2. MODEL 2 — ANOMALOUS COST ESTIMATE DETECTOR (ISOLATION FOREST & PEER IQR/MAD)
================================================================================

OBJECTIVE:
Identify statistically unusual sanctioned/disbursed cost patterns relative to peer works using 
deterministic checks, peer-relative IQR/MAD analysis, and unsupervised Isolation Forest.

CONSENSUS MULTI-SIGNAL ARCHITECTURE:
Model 2 integrates three independent detection signals:

SIGNAL 1: Deterministic Cost Overrun Checker
  - Formula: Disbursed Expenditure > 1.10 * Sanctioned Amount
  - Flagged: Works exceeding approved sanction limit by > 10%.

SIGNAL 2: Hierarchical Peer-Group Statistical IQR & MAD Detector
  - Peer Grouping: Formed dynamically at (State x Work Category) level. Fallback to (National x Work Category)
    if the peer group contains fewer than 10 completed works.
  - Metrics:
      * Interquartile Range (IQR = Q3 - Q1)
      * Median Absolute Deviation (MAD = median(|x - median(x)|))
  - Anomaly Condition: Disbursed Amount > Upper Fence (Q3 + 1.5 * IQR) AND > (Median + 3.0 * MAD).

SIGNAL 3: Unsupervised Isolation Forest Machine Learning Anomaly Detector
  - Algorithm: Scikit-Learn Isolation Forest / Fallback Multivariate Distance Model.
  - Hyperparameters:
      * N Estimators: 300 Isolation Trees
      * Contamination Rate: 0.05 (5% target anomaly distribution)
      * Random State: 42 (Reproducible hyperplanes)
  - Feature Matrix:
      1. Log Sanction Amount: ln(1 + Sanction Amount)
      2. Log Disbursed Amount: ln(1 + Disbursed Amount)
      3. Disbursed-to-Sanctioned Ratio: Disbursed / Sanctioned
      4. Category Z-Score: (Sanction Amount - Peer Mean) / Peer StdDev
  - Mechanism: Recursively partitions data using random feature split lines. Outliers require fewer 
    splits to isolate near tree roots, yielding lower average path length and higher anomaly scores (0 to 1).

CONSENSUS RISK CLASSIFICATION:
  - HIGH RISK: Signal 1 Fired OR (Signal 2 AND Signal 3 Fired)
  - MEDIUM RISK: Signal 2 Fired OR Signal 3 Fired
  - LOW RISK: No Signals Fired

PRODUCTION METRICS (LS 18th Data):
  • High Risk Works: 1,643
  • Medium Risk Works: 4,934
  • Low Risk Works: 72,639

3. MODEL 3 — DELAYED PROJECTS & SLA BREACH DETECTOR (PEER-RELATIVE TUKEY IQR)
================================================================================

OBJECTIVE:
Identify works with abnormal approval-to-completion or sanction-to-execution timelines using non-parametric 
peer statistical distributions.

ALGORITHMIC METHODOLOGY:
- Elapsed Duration Calculation:
    * Completed Works: Duration (days) = Completion Date - Sanction Date
    * Ongoing Works: Duration (days) = Reference Date - Sanction Date
- Dynamic Peer Fencing:
    * Grouping: (State x Work Category)
    * Upper IQR Fence: Threshold = Q3 + 1.5 * IQR
- Classification:
    * Flagged as Delayed if Elapsed Duration > Peer Group Upper IQR Fence.
    * Missing sanction dates are retained under "UNKNOWN_MISSING_SANCTION_DATE" rather than dropped.

PRODUCTION METRICS (LS 18th Data):
  • Completed Works Evaluated: 42,443
  • Ongoing Works Evaluated: 36,778
  • Delayed Completed Works: 714
  • Delayed Ongoing Works: 997
  • Total Delayed Works Flagged: 1,711

5. VENDOR–AGENCY NETWORK RISK ANALYZER (GRAPH & CONCENTRATION METRICS)
================================================================================

OBJECTIVE:
Analyze expenditure transactions to identify monopolistic vendor concentration and small-value payment fragmentation patterns.

NETWORK METRICS:
1. Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI):
   - Formula: HHI = SUM( (Agency_Vendor_Disbursement / Agency_Total_Disbursement)^2 )
   - Threshold: HHI > 0.25 flags high expenditure concentration with a single vendor.

2. 7-Day Payment Fragmentation Window:
   - Scans transaction logs for multiple small-value disbursements issued to the same vendor within 7 calendar days by the same agency.

3. Government Entity Safeguard:
   - Classifies public engineering departments (e.g. District Engineer, Nirmithi Kendra, Zilla Parishad)
     as "GOVERNMENT_ENTITY". Prevents public sector execution from being misidentified as private vendor risk.

PRODUCTION METRICS (LS 18th Data):
  • High Concentration Agencies (HHI > 0.25): 67
  • Payment Structuring Candidates: 651 agencies
  • Whitelisted Government Entities: 34

7. MODEL 5 — AUDIT PRIORITY AGGREGATOR (MULTI-SIGNAL CONSENSUS ENGINE)
================================================================================

OBJECTIVE:
Synthesize signals across Models 1, 2, 3, 4, Vendor Risk, Forecasting, Inadmissible Works, Private Beneficiaries,
Duplicate Expenditure, and Fund Utilization into a normalized audit priority score for operational auditors.

DIMENSION DEFINITIONS:
- MAJOR DIMENSIONS (Configured Dimension Weight >= 0.20):
    1. Cost Risk Dimension (Weight: 0.30)
    2. Speed & Delay Dimension (Weight: 0.25)
    3. Statutory Compliance Dimension (Weight: 0.25)
- SUPPORTING DIMENSIONS (Configured Dimension Weight < 0.20):
    4. Vendor & Payment Dimension (Weight: 0.10)
    5. Eligibility & Beneficiary Dimension (Weight: 0.10)

NORMALIZED WEIGHT DISTRIBUTION:
  • Cost Risk Dimension: 0.30 (High Cost Anomaly) / 0.10 (Medium Cost Anomaly)
  • Speed & Delay Dimension: 0.25 (Model 3 Delay OR Module 7 Idle Utilization)
  • Statutory Compliance Dimension: 0.25 (Model 4 Severe/Moderate Deviation) / 0.10 (Minor Deviation)
  • Vendor Network & Payment Risk Dimension: 0.10 (HHI > 0.25 OR Structuring OR Module 6 Duplicate Exp)
  • Eligibility & Beneficiary Dimension: 0.10 (Module 2 Inadmissible OR Module 3 Private Beneficiary)
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  WEIGHT_SUM = 0.30 + 0.25 + 0.25 + 0.10 + 0.10 = 1.00

PRIORITY SCORE CALCULATION:
  Priority Score = min(1.00, max(0.00, Cost_Score + Delay_Score + Compliance_Score + Vendor_Score + Eligibility_Score))
  (Strictly mathematically bounded 0.00 <= Priority Score <= 1.00)

AUTHORITATIVE DETERMINISTIC TIER ASSIGNMENT RULE:
- CRITICAL_AUDIT_PRIORITY:
    misuse_priority_score >= 0.50 OR major_dimension_count >= 2
    (Requires at least two MAJOR dimensions with weight >= 0.20 to be fired. Supporting dimensions alone cannot trigger critical escalation).
- STANDARD_REVIEW:
    0.20 <= misuse_priority_score < 0.50 OR major_dimension_count >= 1
- LOW_PRIORITY:
    misuse_priority_score < 0.20 and major_dimension_count == 0
